## Direction contract

THESIS: The gift list is a continuation of Paloma & Guilherme's own chá de panela invitation, not a generic online registry skin. It refuses the category's default look for this kind of page (flat white cards, a single bright accent, corporate checklist tone).

OWN-WORLD: Deep olive (#454E2E, radial-vignette toward #3B4326 at the edges) is the structural/chrome color — hero, sticky nav rail, footer. Warm ivory (#FAF6EC) is the working surface for the item grid, so 60+ repeated cards stay scannable. Soft sage (#8B9470 / #E7EAD6 tint) is the secondary accent for badges, active states, and the claimed state (claimed leans into the brand's own olive-green rather than a foreign forest green). Alex Brush (script) carries the couple's names and the event name at hero scale only. Cormorant Garamond carries headings and category titles. Lora carries body copy, item names, and every UI control — chosen for on-screen legibility over the invitation's more decorative serif, since the audience includes less tech-fluent, older relatives reading on phones. A hand-authored heart glyph (filled, small) is the recurring "structural division / chosen-with-love" mark: it opens and closes the hero as a heart-flanked rule, and prefixes the claimed badge. A hand-authored olive-branch sprig anchors the hero's corners. A thin cream corner-bracket frame (not a full border), with one curved flourish top-right, delineates the hero card, echoing the reference invitation's frame. A soft, irregular sage brushstroke highlights "Paloma & Guilherme" the way the reference highlights the couple's names. A hand-drawn line illustration of a pot, whisk and spoon (with a small heart on the pot) closes the page in the footer, tying back to "chá de panela" itself. No kicker/eyebrow line sits above either script headline (craft-floor ban, holds even though the reference itself uses tracked labels above its headlines) — the heart-rule dividers do that structural job instead.

STORY: A guest opens the link and immediately recognizes "their" invitation's look and feel. They read the couple's message, then move past the hero into a calm, legible, easy-to-scan ivory grid where the ornament recedes so they can find an item and claim it in seconds — including on a phone, including as a relative who isn't especially online-savvy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed deep-olive hero, corner-bracket frame, sprig in each corner. Centered: heart-flanked rule → "Chá de Panela" in Alex Brush at hero scale → "Paloma & Guilherme" in Alex Brush, smaller, on the sage brushstroke → the existing welcome message in Lora → a sage progress pill with a heart → heart-flanked rule closing the hero. Below the fold the page shifts onto the ivory surface: sticky olive/ivory pill category nav, category sections in Cormorant Garamond, item cards in white-on-ivory with olive-family borders and a claim button that inverts from olive-outline to filled olive-on-hover.

FORM: Directly pinned by a real photo the couple supplied of their own printed chá de panela invitation — not drawn from a concept roll. concept-seed's tournament was not run: the direction arrived as a concrete, already-approved reference asset (the couple's actual event collateral) stronger than any candidate the roll could generate, and running a dice-roll tournament against the client's own real artifact would manufacture alternatives nobody wanted. This substitution is disclosed here per the skill's degraded-path convention. Seed key: none (pinned reference stands in place of a seed). No image-generation tool is available in this session/harness, so the build is code-led by necessity, not choice; all identity marks (heart, sprig, pot/whisk/spoon) are hand-authored inline SVG line art rather than generated rasters, matching the reference's own monoline illustration style.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance (no rasters ship in this build — every mark is authored vector line art, so provenance is the authored SVG source itself).
